## Board definitions

- Files: [app/src/editor/boards/data/](app/src/editor/boards/data/) (96 boards, pins used by the editor pin picker)
  and the generated index [app/src/editor/boards/index.ts](app/src/editor/boards/index.ts)
- Source: [gablau/tinkerblink-boards](https://github.com/gablau/tinkerblink-boards), a fork of
  [blynkkk/boards](https://github.com/blynkkk/boards) (commit `7982404`, 2020-06-26), the board description
  files of the Blynk platform. Imported with [app/scripts/import-boards.js](app/scripts/import-boards.js): the
  files are minified, spaces in file names become `_`, and the `debug/` boards are left out.
- Authors: Blynk Inc. (the first 66 boards, unchanged); Gabriele Lauricella (30 boards added in the fork,
  each with a `based_on` link to the board documentation)
- License: MIT, full text in [app/src/editor/boards/data/LICENSE](app/src/editor/boards/data/LICENSE)
